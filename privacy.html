Privacy policy

Alai is a personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy covers the Google account data it can access.

What Alai accesses

  • Gmail: reads and searches the owner's email, and sends email only when the owner approves each message.
  • Google Calendar: reads the owner's calendars, and adds or deletes events only when the owner approves each change.

How it is used

This data is used only to answer the owner's own requests. It is processed on the owner's computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. A request is sent to an outside AI service only when the owner reviews and approves exactly what will be sent.

Storage and removal

Sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Alai's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
